## Verification protocol
1. Cite both the **asset download/catalog page** and the relevant **license terms**.
2. State the date and what page said; don't copy large passages of other people's content.
3. Record exceptions such as model-release restrictions, embedded third-party textures, attribution files, download accounts, or no redistribution.
4. Do not invent direct-download URLs, file sizes, model counts or measurements.
5. Mark link dead, license conflicting, or no original-source proof as **unverified** and avoid approved recommendations.
