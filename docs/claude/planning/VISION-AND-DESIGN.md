# Daybreak Row — future vision and design alternatives

**Status: NOT STARTED.** The current game mechanics in the canonical handoff are facts; everything here should be **Claude's proposed direction**, not an asserted owner decision.

## What the game is today
See [CURRENT-STATE](../../handoff/CURRENT-STATE.md). The game is currently a mobile-first coastal/city life simulation with economy, needs, resident schedules, daily passport and eight interiors.

## Candidate directions to evaluate
Propose at least two substantially different long-term visions. Illustrative starting questions (not requirements):
- A living, relationship-driven city with deeper routines, jobs, homes and emergent daily stories?
- A broader exploration and community-simulation game with player progression, events and meaningful city districts?
- A primarily photoreal urban sandbox, if feasible on the actual mobile hardware?

For each: audience, unique proposition, core daily/weekly gameplay loops, strengths, weaknesses, asset needs, engineering risk, estimated complexity and how it preserves the current game's identity.

## Recommended direction
After comparing alternatives, give a reasoned preferred direction with a concise one-paragraph vision. Distinguish desirable visual realism from unrealistic resource use on the target phone.

## System pillars and coherence
Discuss where the gameplay is currently shallow and what deepens it: movement, interactions, residents/relationships, economy, progression, activities/jobs, diverse spaces, sound, polish, accessibility, UI/UX and eventual distribution. Distinguish **critical foundation** from novelty features.

## Unknowns for owner approval
List explicit product decisions that research alone cannot settle, with sensible defaults and trade-offs, before suggesting implementation.

## Guardrails
Protect current saves and touch controls, keep fixed daytime visuals as a current preference, and do not assume multiplayer, generative NPC APIs, servers or monetization have been authorized.
